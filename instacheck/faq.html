Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to manual unfollow users on Instagram? ▼

Yes, as long as you adhere to Instagram's speed limits. To prevent temporary action blocks, do not unfollow more than 10–20 accounts per hour manually. Automated unfollow bots will trigger security blocks.

Why does InstaCheck state 0 followers after I upload? ▼

Make sure when requesting your archive from Instagram Meta Accounts Center that you selected "Connections" data. If you downloaded media only, follower files will not exist in the archive.

Is my Instagram data saved anywhere? ▼

No. All data processing occurs strictly in your device's browser memory (RAM). Once you close or refresh this webpage, all data is permanently cleared.
