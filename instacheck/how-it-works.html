Step-by-Step Guide: Exporting Your Instagram Data

1 Open Accounts Center

In your Instagram mobile app or desktop browser, navigate to Settings & Privacy > Accounts Center > Your Information and Permissions.

2 Download Your Information

Select Download Your Information, then click Request a Download. Choose "Some of your information" to speed up processing.

3 Select Followers & Following

Scroll down and select Connections > Followers and Following. Set Format to JSON and Date Range to All Time.Make sure to set media Quality High for accurate result.

4 Upload .ZIP to InstaCheck

Once Meta emails you the download link (takes ~5 minutes), download the .zip file to your device and drag it directly into InstaCheck.
